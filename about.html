The Beginning

Born From
Conviction

Maison Noire was founded in Paris in 2009 by creative director Élise Moreau. Having worked for a decade in the atelier of two Parisian couture houses, Élise grew disillusioned with fashion's obsession with noise — louder prints, more embellishment, faster cycles.

She left to build something quieter, and more lasting. Maison Noire's first collection — twelve pieces, all in black, ivory, and stone — sold out in three days through a single editorial in Le Monde.

Our Materials

Fabric is
Everything

We work exclusively with natural fibres: linen from Belgium, cashmere from Mongolia, silk from Como, merino from New Zealand. Each is selected by our founder personally, hand-felt at the source.

We maintain long-term relationships with fewer than twelve suppliers. This is not efficiency — it is fidelity. We believe great fabric requires trust built over years.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Timelessness

We design for a wardrobe that outlasts every trend. A Maison Noire piece should feel as relevant in ten years as it does today.

Integrity

From seed to sale, we demand full transparency in our supply chain. We never compromise on ethics, even when it costs more.

Craftsmanship

Every seam is a statement. We work with artisans who have spent lifetimes mastering their craft — and we pay them accordingly.
